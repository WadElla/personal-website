abstract: Revelation is an AI-agent framework for interpreting IoT packet captures at multiple levels of evidence rather than reducing traces to coarse detection labels. It converts raw PCAPs into protocol logs, packet-level records, flow summaries, anomaly reports, and targeted threat-intelligence context, then indexes these artifacts for retrieval-augmented question answering. The agent grounds its responses in retrieved traffic evidence, helping operators explain benign behavior, exfiltration-like transfers, backdoor activity, and HTTP-flood behavior with clearer links between observations and conclusions.
